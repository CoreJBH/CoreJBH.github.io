Privacy Policy

App: PocketCore — Bitcoin Core full node for Android · Package com.corejbh.pocketcore · Developer CoreJBH · Effective 21 November 2026

The short version

PocketCore collects no personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no third-party tracking SDK of any kind. Everything the app knows stays on your phone.

What the app does not collect

  • No personal information. PocketCore never asks for, reads or transmits your name, email address, phone number, contacts, calendar, photos, files, precise location, advertising ID or any other identifier.
  • No analytics or telemetry. There is no usage tracking, no event logging, no session recording and no A/B experimentation library in the app.
  • No crash reports. If the app or the node stops, nothing is uploaded. Logs and debug.log remain in the app's private storage on the device.
  • No advertising and no tracking SDKs. The app contains no ad network, no attribution SDK, no social login SDK and no push notification service. It links to no third-party service at runtime other than the Bitcoin peer-to-peer network described below.
  • No wallet and no keys. PocketCore is a node, not a wallet. It does not create, store or accept private keys, seed phrases or WIF strings, and the bundled daemon is built with ENABLE_WALLET=OFF.

Network connections the app makes

PocketCore makes exactly two kinds of connection, both to do the one job the app has — validating the Bitcoin blockchain itself.

  • Bitcoin peer-to-peer. The bundled Bitcoin Core daemon connects directly to other Bitcoin nodes to download block headers and blocks and to relay transactions, using Bitcoin's encrypted BIP324 v2 transport where the peer supports it. This is the same traffic any Bitcoin node produces: IP addresses of peers, protocol messages, and the public blockchain data itself. It is not sent to the developer or to any server the developer controls, and the app sends no device identifiers along with it.
  • Loopback RPC. The app talks to its own daemon over 127.0.0.1 with a randomly generated username and password that never leave the device. The RPC port is bound to loopback only and is not reachable from the LAN or the internet.

The app makes no HTTP requests of its own. Android's network security configuration allows cleartext traffic to 127.0.0.1 and localhost only; every other destination would have to be HTTPS.

What is stored on your device

All data stays in the app's private storage, readable only by PocketCore:

  • the validated blockchain data (headers, blocks, chainstate),
  • bitcoin.conf and the daemon's logs,
  • your app settings (network, prune size, watch-only descriptor) in app-private SharedPreferences,
  • the generated RPC password, sealed with an Android Keystore key.

None of it is backed up or synchronized anywhere. android:allowBackup="false" keeps it out of Android's cloud backup.

A watch-only descriptor is used only to derive addresses locally so the node can search its own UTXO set for them; it is never transmitted to a third party.

Permissions and why they are needed

PermissionWhy
INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATEconnect to Bitcoin peers
FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USEkeep the node running while the screen is off
POST_NOTIFICATIONSshow the node status card (height, sync progress)
WAKE_LOCKlet the CPU finish block validation with the screen off

No permission is used to read your personal data, and none is requested for contacts, storage, location, camera or microphone.

Children

PocketCore is a technical tool for running a Bitcoin node. It is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Data deletion

Because nothing is collected, there is nothing for the developer to delete. To remove all data the app has created, uninstall it; Android deletes the app's private storage, including the synced chain, with it.

Changes to this policy

If the app's behaviour ever changes, this document will be updated in the same repository before the change ships, with the effective date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the app: open an issue at github.com/CoreJBH/pocketcore/issues, or use the developer contact address shown on the PocketCore listing in Google Play.
